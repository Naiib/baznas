- Had Kifayah formula, custom COA, new approval ceilings, and unapproved PSAK 109 policy: BLOCKED; no values or rules are inferred.
- Persistent staging CRUD and preview deployment: BLOCKED pending authorized staging access and the separately authorized preview/service change. No production DB/VPS, deploy, push, or service action was performed.
- DOCX export and full role/action regression: not evidenced by current source/tests; remain PARTIAL/FAIL requirements rather than implied complete.
